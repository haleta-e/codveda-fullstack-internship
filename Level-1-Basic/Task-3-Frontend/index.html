About Menged

Bridging Ethiopian Tech Talent with Global Opportunities

Menged (መንገድ) translates to "Path" or "Way" in Amharic. The platform was created to address a critical challenge faced by Ethiopian university students and recent graduates: lack of centralized, accessible, and verified career pathways.

By consolidating internships, scholarships, remote developer positions, intensive bootcamps, and leadership fellowships into a single structured discovery platform, Menged accelerates the transition from university study to successful careers.
